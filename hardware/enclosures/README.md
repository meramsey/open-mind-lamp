# Enclosure guidance

Use the manufacturer's [official 3D model](https://files.waveshare.com/wiki/ESP32-S3-Matrix/esp32-s3-matrix-3d.zip)
to check fit against your physical revision. No unverified printable enclosure is
shipped as mechanically qualified. Allow access to BOOT, RESET and USB-C, cable
strain relief, ventilation on both faces, nonconductive standoffs and clearance
between the LEDs and a frosted diffuser. Validate temperature and supply current
in the final enclosure at the intended brightness. Do not bury the powered board
in resin or foam. The board's IMU could support future orientation-aware modes.
